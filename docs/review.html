6 RESEARCH STAGES
01 · START

A shorter introduction, then availability

INTRO Short welcome → W1 “Do you have about 5 minutes now?” → yes: C00 sign-in → W2 research consent.

↳ Later → CB: email, date, time and time zone → one scheduled email reminder (planned). No or no consent → DECLINED. A reminder does not require sign-in.

02 · ABOUT YOU

Online buying and eligibility

S1 Comfortable buying online? → maybe/no: S1b Why want a person? → S1bv Optional detail → S2 Quote in last six months?

↳ S2 = no → SCREENED_OUT.

03 · THE QUOTE

What happened when they saw it?

Q3 Source → Q4 Ease (1–5) → Q5 First reaction (voice/type) → Q6 Understanding of cover → Q7 “When you saw the quote, how sure were you about buying that plan?”

↳ Very unsure · A little unsure · Not sure · Fairly sure · Very sure.

04 · HESITATION

What held them back?

H8 Hesitated? → yes: H9 Up to three reasons as tiles: Cost and timing / Cover and claims / Comparing and trust / Something else → H9v Optional details → H10 What they actually did.

↳ H8 = no → skip to P11. Choice values are unchanged.

05 · PAYMENT

What did they decide?

P11 Paid / deciding / stopped → paid: P12a What tipped it? and P12c “When you paid, how sure were you that you understood what the plan would cover?” → otherwise: P12b What would have made you pay?

↳ P12c now measures understanding of cover, not vague confidence in payment.

06 · WRAP-UP

What was missing — and optional email follow-up

X13 “What else, if anything, would have helped you understand or decide about that quote?” → X14 Email a one-page guide, future study findings, both or neither → X14c Email if requested → Q21 Finish → THANKS.

↳ No WhatsApp, referrals or generic thank-you email. Guide and findings cannot be promised live until their contents and delivery are ready.

New voice behavior

One-tap recording with up to two minutes and optional neutral prompts (“Take your time” or “Anything else that stood out?”). After recording, show only a short “I understood” interpretation and move on automatically. No raw transcript shown and no mandatory second confirmation. Back still allows corrections.
