THE ROADMAP

Seven steps to a launch-ready survey

Each phase has a deliverable and a review gate. We will not connect live respondent data before you approve the flow.

Connect a new Mumbai-region Supabase project, run reviewed migrations, enable RLS and private audio storage, test least-privilege access.

AFTER APPROVAL

Production API and sign-in

Deploy the existing Node/Vercel endpoints; configure Google sign-in, contact alternative, session passes, retries and auto-resume.

05
AFTER APPROVAL

Sarvam voice and read-back

Use Sarvam only through the server. Implement two-minute recording, 29-second clips, transcription, editable answers and equal-weight confirmation.

Deliverable: voice-first flow with typing parity
06
BEFORE LAUNCH

Accessibility, security and device tests

Audit permissions, secrets, storage grants, final-save states, iPhone microphone failures, 320px layouts and automated routes.

Deliverable: passed test matrix and key-free repository
07
RELEASE GATE

Publish the real survey

Set production environment variables, confirm guide and callback fulfillment, review the privacy policy, then share the live URL.

Deliverable: approved live application and handoff

What to test

Use Back to change a response and inspect how the later questions change.

  • “Maybe” on online buying opens the person-support branch.
  • “No” on recent quote ends at a screen-out.
  • Hesitation opens a three-choice blocker question.
  • Payment changes the next question.
  • Voice questions use an editable, simulated read-back.

A completed demo does not mean a response was saved.

DECISION GATE · YOUR REVIEW

Review the complete survey flow.

Built from the approved source branch structure. These are the research screens to validate before production implementation.

6 RESEARCH STAGES
01 · START

Introduction, sign-in and consent

INTRO Welcome and why it matters → C00 Google or contact sign-in → W1 Do you have 5 minutes? → W2 Consent.

↳ W1 = later → CB callback form → CALLBACK. No, decline or no consent → DECLINED.

02 · ABOUT YOU

Buying comfort and eligibility

S1 Comfortable purchasing online? → if maybe/no: S1b Why a person? → S1bv Optional tell-me-more → S2 Quote in last six months?

↳ S2 = no → SCREENED_OUT; otherwise continue.

03 · THE QUOTE

Source and first reaction

Q3 Where quote came from → Q4 Ease (1–5) → Q5 Initial reaction (voice/type) → Q6 Understood coverage? → Q7 Readiness (1–5).

04 · HESITATION

What got in the way?

H8 Hesitated? → if yes: H9 Up to three blockers → H9v Optional detail (voice/type) → H10 What did you actually do?

↳ H8 = no → skip straight to P11.

05 · PAYMENT

What happened next?

P11 Paid / deciding / stopped → paid: P12a What tipped it? and P12c Confidence (1–5); otherwise P12b What would have made you pay?

06 · WRAP-UP

One improvement and close

X13 One thing to change (voice/type) → X14 Free one-page guide? → if yes: X14c Delivery contact → Q21 Finish → THANKS.

↳ Hold X14 for real launch until the guide exists and a sending method is approved.
