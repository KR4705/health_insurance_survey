ABOUT THE PROJECT

A study of the decision after the quote.

This is an independent student research project run by Rohith as part of an Airtribe AI-First Product Management capstone. It explores how people experience health insurance quotes: how they received them, what they understood, what made them hesitate, and what they eventually did.

The planned survey takes around five minutes. Respondents will be able to speak or type open answers, see a short non-blocking “I understood” note, and go back to revise an answer. The spoken transcript will not be shown on screen.

This project is not operated by or affiliated with an insurance company. We won't offer insurance advice, influence buying decisions, or sell your answers.
