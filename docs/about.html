ABOUT THIS STUDY

Why we're asking.

I'm Rohith, a product management student. I'm studying what people experienced after they got a price for health insurance: what was clear, what made them hesitate and what helped them decide.

This is an independent student project, part of an Airtribe AI-First Product Management capstone. It is not an insurer's survey and does not give insurance or medical advice.

The planned survey takes around five minutes. People can speak or type, whichever is easier. Your experience is valuable whether you bought a plan or decided not to.
