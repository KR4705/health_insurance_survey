PRIVACY · DESIGN REVIEW ONLY

This version is a non-collecting prototype.

The GitHub Pages review template demonstrates screen layouts, a one-time email reminder request and revised answer flows. It does not submit answers, audio, contact details or events to any database, model provider or server. It does not use Google sign-in or request microphone access. Your inputs exist only in the current page session and are cleared on reload.

For the planned production survey, the privacy design is to collect survey answers and only the contact information needed for sign-in, one reminder email, a one-page guide or requested aggregated study findings; store contact details separately from answers; hide speech transcripts from respondent screens while securely retaining answers for research, and automatically delete uploaded audio after successful final submission; restrict all response reads; and not sell or share research answers. Those protections are not a claim that the production service already exists. A fully reviewed privacy notice will replace this page before collection begins.

Please do not enter real policy numbers, health details, or personal contact information in this demo. Use imaginary details when reviewing the screens.
