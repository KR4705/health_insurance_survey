Your words stay with you in this preview.

What happens in this demo?

Nothing you enter is sent to a survey server, database, email service or AI provider. The demo does not use your microphone or real Google sign-in. Answers are held only in this page's memory and are cleared when you reload it.

Please use made-up contact details when testing. Do not include a policy number, health details or anyone else's personal information.

What will change for the live study?

The proposed live survey will ask for consent, collect answers, and process voice privately. Audio recordings are intended to be deleted after successful final submission. Contact details will be separated from answers and used only for sign-in or requested email follow-ups: one reminder, a guide or study findings.

The full privacy policy, retention periods, security checks and working email delivery must be completed before any public launch. This page is not a final production privacy notice.
